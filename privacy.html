VidStudio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What the app accesses

VidStudio uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's consent it requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos.
  • youtube: to set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, schedules and playlists, and to read basic statistics of the owner's own channels.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth tokens and all data the app reads are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are used only to manage the owner's own channels.

Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising. The app has no servers and uses no analytics or cookies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files removes all stored data.

Third-party terms

By using VidStudio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
